Know where it goes

Sorting waste is easier than you think.

Keep useful materials in circulation and prevent litter by separating household waste. Follow the guidance of your local collection service for accepted items.

🍃

Biodegradable

Food scraps, fruit and vegetable peels, and yard trimmings. Keep them separate for composting where available.

♻️

Recyclable

Clean, dry paper, cardboard, glass, metal cans, and accepted plastic containers. Check local recycling rules.

🧤

Special waste

Batteries, bulbs, electronics, and other potentially hazardous household items need careful handling. Never mix them with food waste.

🗑️

Residual

Items that cannot be reused, composted, or recycled through available local programs. Reduce these where you can.
