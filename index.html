Know where it goes

Sorting waste is easier than you think.

Keep useful materials in circulation and prevent litter by separating waste at home, at school, and wherever you go.

🍃

Biodegradable

Food scraps, fruit and vegetable peels, and yard trimmings. Keep them separate for composting where available.

Tip: Avoid mixing in plastic or other packaging.
♻️

Recyclable

Clean, dry paper, cardboard, glass, metal cans, and accepted plastic containers.

Tip: Rinse containers and check local acceptance.
🧤

Special waste

Batteries, bulbs, electronics, and other potentially hazardous household items need careful handling.

Tip: Don't put these in regular mixed waste.
🗑️

Residual

Items that cannot be reused, composted, or recycled through available local programs.

Tip: Reduce single-use items whenever possible.

Our place, our responsibility

Make cleaner habits part of everyday life.

EcoSort Daet is a community-focused information website concept for residents, students, and local businesses. Start with one habit: bring a reusable bag, separate food scraps, or keep recyclables clean and dry.

Local information: Collection days, accepted recyclables, and special-waste drop-off arrangements may change. Please confirm current details with your barangay or the Municipality of Daet before setting waste out or bringing items to a facility.
